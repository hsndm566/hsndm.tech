# English UI, Saudi Arabia, and Localisation Audit Findings

All visible English service references were checked for regional accuracy. The public positioning, selection controls, campaign copy, review locations, Jeddah directions, search metadata, social descriptions, and sitemap consistently specify Saudi Arabia. English and Arabic are the only service languages represented. No legacy Gulf, Somali, or Filipino claims were found in current client-facing page content.

The Arabic route was checked for its Saudi Arabia references and core translation accuracy. Its Arabic copy correctly identifies a Saudi Arabia-focused service, names Arabic and English support, and uses an RTL composition. Following the requested scope, substantial Arabic design and copy enhancement has been deferred while the English experience received the current refinement pass.

The English home page was rechecked at desktop and 375px mobile widths. The hero now foregrounds a visible application queue and workflow panel, headings use a more disciplined operational display style, matching preferences retain their responsive layout, and the sticky mobile campaign route remains available. The broader campaign, confirmation, and recovery routes retained their existing coherent operational treatment during this focused English update.
