Process Optimization at King Abdulaziz International Airport

This capstone project applied Lean Management and the DMAIC framework to the passenger check-in process at KAIA Terminal 1. The goal: cut processing time, reduce queues, and balance workload.

Approach

Using the DMAIC cycle, I mapped the current-state process with Value Stream Mapping, ran time-and-motion studies, then piloted interventions — self-service kiosks, digital ID verification, and dynamic counter allocation.

Key Results

  • Average total check-in time dropped from 15.5 to 9.2 minutes.
  • Queue waiting time was roughly halved.
  • Document verification time fell by 57%.
  • Off-peak counter utilization improved to 70%.
