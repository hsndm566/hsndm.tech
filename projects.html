Process Optimization at King Abdulaziz International Airport

Applied Lean Management & DMAIC to the Terminal 1 check-in process — goal: cut processing time, shrink queues, balance workload. Mapped current state with VSM, ran time-and-motion studies, piloted self-service kiosks, digital ID verification and dynamic counter allocation.
