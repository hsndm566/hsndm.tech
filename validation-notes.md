# Visual Verification Notes

Desktop and mobile full-page previews were reviewed after implementation.

The desktop composition preserves the intended **Operational Clarity** direction: the dark hero establishes hierarchy, the warm paper sections create editorial breathing room, and signal vermilion remains reserved for priority and action. The status ledger, process rail, pricing comparison, and final campaign prompt are visibly distinct without relying on generic card patterns.

The mobile composition keeps the core journey in a readable sequence. The hero retains a visible CTA and operational indicators, capability blocks stack cleanly, pricing cards preserve plan contrast, and the FAQ and final WhatsApp path remain accessible. No visual defects were observed in the reviewed previews.

The independent visual review also found the design aligned with the selected direction and did not identify required revision items.
