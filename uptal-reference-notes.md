# Uptal Reference Findings

Source reviewed: https://uptal.com/

The reference site uses a feature switcher that lets visitors move among three clear service modes, a large explanatory video area, a simple slider interaction tied to a concrete estimate, and strong English/Arabic support. It also separates conversion paths for employers and job seekers.

For AutoApply SA, the appropriate adaptations are a compact, accessible process switcher inside the existing campaign preview and a dedicated video-ready hero layer. Uptal’s enterprise-hiring claims, company logos, copy, numeric statistics, and brand visual language will not be reused because they do not represent AutoApply SA’s candidate-focused Saudi Arabia service.
