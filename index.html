01 / How it works

A job search with a clear operating system.

Less portal repetition. More focused applications. You keep control over what goes out under your name.

STEP 01↘

Set the direction

Share your CV, target roles, cities, industries, seniority, and application language.

STEP 02↘

We find and prepare

Relevant openings are matched to your profile and prepared with the context the role actually needs.

STEP 03↘

You approve the next step

Review what was prepared, request changes or skip it, then approve before submission.

02 / Product

Built to feel like a workspace, not a black box.

The useful part of automation is not hiding the process. It is reducing repetitive work while keeping the important decisions visible.

YOUR CONTROL LAYER

Match → prepare → review → track.

AutoApply SA is structured around an approval-led workflow so you can see what the system is doing and why a role belongs in your campaign.

  • Target by Saudi city and industry
  • Review prepared applications before submission
  • Keep an application record you can return to
  • Use English or Arabic campaign support

03 / Who it’s for

For people who need momentum without the mess.

The service is most useful when the problem is not motivation—it is the amount of repetitive application work between you and enough good opportunities.

FIRST ROLE

New graduates

Build a steady application rhythm while you focus on interviews, networking, and strengthening your profile.

CAREER MOVE

Professionals changing direction

Explore a new role lane, industry, or city without rebuilding the same application from scratch every night.

BUSY SEARCH

Working candidates

Keep the search moving when you do not have hours each day to monitor portals, tailor documents, and track follow-ups.
